Privacy Policy for RSL Mercy Tracker

Effective Date: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RSL Mercy Tracker ("Shared Minds," "we," "our," or "the App") handles your information. Your privacy is a priority, and we are committed to being transparent about the data we use.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Data

RSL Mercy Tracker does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require account registration, and we do not have access to your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

100% Privacy Focused: Your tracker data stays on your phone. No tracking cookies, no personal accounts, and no data selling.

B. Local Data Storage

The App stores your game-related data (shard pull counts, mercy milestones, and profile names) locally on your device using Android's DataStore. This data is not uploaded to our servers. You can delete this data at any time by:

  1. Deleting individual profiles within the App.
  2. Clearing the App's storage/cache in your device settings.
  3. Uninstalling the App.

C. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. To provide relevant ads and limit ad frequency, Google may collect and use certain non-personal information, including:

  • Advertising ID (AAID): A unique, user-resettable ID for advertising, provided by Google Play services.
  • Device Information: Such as device model, OS version, and network type.
  • Interaction Data: Information about how you interact with ads within the App.

This data is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy & Terms. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

2. Data Usage

The information used by the App is strictly for:

  • Functionality: Keeping track of your shard mercy progression.
  • Monetization: Displaying advertisements to support the development of the App.

3. Data Security

Since your game data is stored locally, its security depends on your device's security. We use standard Android encryption for data in transit (e.g., when syncing champion data from GitHub via HTTPS).

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect personal information from any users, we do not intentionally target children.

5. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to external sites (such as GitHub for updates or documentation). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and, if appropriate, providing a notice within the App.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer via the contact information provided on the App's official Google Play Store listing.
